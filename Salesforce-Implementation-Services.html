The problem we solve
Why implementations fail — and how we prevent it
Most Salesforce implementations underdeliver not because of the platform, but because of what happens around it. These are the three most common failure points we see and fix every day.

Poor upfront planning

Jumping straight to configuration without clear scope leads to rework, delays, and budget overruns. Most failed projects skip this critical phase entirely.

How Atlas prevents this

Every project starts with a structured Discovery phase — agreed scope, fixed milestones, and a delivery plan before a single line of config is written.

No change management

Staff avoid using a new system when they aren't brought on the journey. Low adoption kills ROI faster than any technical issue ever could.

How Atlas prevents this

We run role-based training, hands-on workshops, and post-go-live support so your team uses Salesforce confidently from day one.

Messy data migration

Migrating dirty data just moves the problem. Bad data in Salesforce destroys user trust and reporting accuracy immediately after go-live.

How Atlas prevents this

We audit, cleanse, and validate all data before migration — with a rollback plan in place. Clean, reliable data from day one.
