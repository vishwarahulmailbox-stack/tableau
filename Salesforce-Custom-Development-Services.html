When you need us
When do you need custom development?
Point-and-click won't cut it in every situation. These are the six scenarios where custom Salesforce development delivers what no standard feature can.

Complex pricing or quoting logic

Your pricing rules are too dynamic for CPQ out-of-the-box. A custom pricing engine handles your exact logic — discounts, tiers, bundles, and exceptions.

Unique business processes

Your approval workflows, case routing, or commission calculations are specific to your business and can't be replicated with standard Salesforce flows.

Bespoke user interfaces

Your team needs custom Lightning Web Components — branded portals, specialised data entry screens, or mobile-first experiences the standard UI can't deliver.

High-volume data processing

Standard Salesforce hits governor limits with large data volumes. Custom Apex batch jobs handle millions of records reliably without hitting those walls.

Deep system integrations

You need Salesforce to write back to your ERP, sync with your payment gateway in real-time, or connect to a legacy system via a custom API — not a middleware workaround.

AppExchange product development

Building a managed package to sell on AppExchange? We architect, build, and security-review Salesforce ISV products from the ground up.

Quality & governance
Code quality standards that protect your investment
Custom code that isn't built to a high standard becomes technical debt fast. Every line of code we write is held to strict quality gates — so your org stays healthy, upgradeable, and maintainable for years.

Minimum 90% Apex test coverage

We enforce 90%+ test coverage on all Apex code — well above Salesforce's 75% minimum — so your code behaves predictably in production.

PMD & ESLint code scanning

Every deployment is scanned with PMD for Apex and ESLint for LWC — catching security vulnerabilities, performance issues, and bad patterns before they reach production.

Mandatory peer code review

No code goes to staging without a second developer review. This catches logic errors, improves readability, and shares knowledge across the team.

Full documentation & handover

Every component is documented with technical specifications, deployment instructions, and admin guidance — so your team can maintain it confidently after we're done.

Salesforce Custom Development FAQs
What is Salesforce custom development?
Salesforce custom development refers to building bespoke functionality on top of the Salesforce platform using Apex (Salesforce's programming language), Lightning Web Components (LWC), custom APIs, and other developer tools. It goes beyond what declarative point-and-click tools like Flow or Process Builder can achieve — delivering unique business logic, custom UI, and deep system integrations.
