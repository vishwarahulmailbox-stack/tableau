Risk management
Risks we protect you from
Re-implementation carries real risks — data loss, business disruption, and poor adoption are the most common. Every phase of our approach is designed to eliminate them.

Data loss during migration

Moving data between org configurations is the highest-risk moment in a re-implementation. One mistake can be catastrophic and irreversible.

How Atlas protects you

Every migration includes a full pre-migration backup, validation checks at each stage, and a tested rollback plan before we touch production data.

Business disruption at go-live

A poorly managed cutover can bring sales, service, and operations to a halt. Some organisations experience outages lasting days or weeks.

How Atlas protects you

We plan cutover to the hour — with phased rollouts, a hypercare period, and dedicated support available immediately post-launch to resolve any issues.

Staff reverting to old habits

A technically perfect re-implementation still fails if your team ignores the new system and goes back to spreadsheets within a month of go-live.

How Atlas protects you

Change management and adoption monitoring are built into every engagement. We track adoption KPIs post-launch and intervene if numbers don't improve.
