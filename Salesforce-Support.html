The problem we solve
The real cost of unmanaged Salesforce
Most organisations treat Salesforce support as optional — until something breaks. These are the three most common and costly consequences of leaving your platform unmanaged.

Dirty, unreliable data

Without regular cleansing, duplicates multiply, records go stale, and reports become meaningless. Leadership loses confidence in the numbers and stops using Salesforce for decisions.

How Atlas prevents this

Monthly data quality reviews, automated deduplication rules, and validation maintenance keep your data clean and trustworthy year-round.

Security gaps & compliance risks

Unpatched Salesforce releases, outdated profiles, and unreviewed sharing rules create security vulnerabilities that put your business data — and your clients' data — at risk.

How Atlas prevents this

We apply every Salesforce release proactively, audit security settings quarterly, and keep your org compliant with current best practices throughout the year.

Low adoption & user frustration

When users hit errors, broken flows, or missing features with no-one to call, they stop using Salesforce. Low adoption snowballs — and the investment you've made goes to waste.

How Atlas prevents this

Our dedicated helpdesk handles user queries fast, we monitor adoption metrics monthly, and we run targeted retraining when usage drops below threshold.
